The house

Quiet pieces,
made to be kept.

Neginsara begins with the stone. Each piece is drawn around a single centre — its cut, its colour, the way it holds the light — and everything else is kept deliberately out of the way.

Settings are fine, lines are clean, and nothing is added for the sake of being noticed. These are pieces for people who already know what they like.

The name is Persian, and it is the whole brief: a single stone, set well, with nothing added that it does not need.

We keep our designs few so that each can be considered properly — from the proportion of a halo to the weight of a chain against the collarbone.

Pieces that look as right in twenty years as they do today.
